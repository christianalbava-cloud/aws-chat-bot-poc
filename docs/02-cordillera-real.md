# Trekking in the Cordillera Real

The Cordillera Real is a mountain range in the Bolivian Andes that extends northeast of La Paz, with several peaks exceeding 6,000 masl.

## Condoriri Trek

Multi-day circuit around the Condoriri group of peaks, featuring turquoise-colored glacial lagoons.

- **Duration**: 3 to 5 days, depending on the chosen circuit.
- **Maximum trek altitude**: around 5,000 masl at the mountain passes.
- **Starting point**: Tuni community, about 2 hours from La Paz.
- **Difficulty**: high, requires proper prior acclimatization.

## Huayna Potosí Ascent

Although technically a mountain climb (not a simple trek), it's one of the most popular activities for visitors to La Paz.

- **Altitude**: 6,088 masl.
- **Typical duration**: 2 days with one night at a high base camp.
- **Requires**: technical mountaineering gear (crampons, ice axe, harness) and a certified guide.

## Sorata - Laguna Chillata - Laguna Glaciar Route

Multi-day trek in the Sorata area, at the foot of the Illampu snow-capped peak.

- **Duration**: 2 to 4 days.
- **Difficulty**: medium-high.
- **Starting point**: town of Sorata, about 3-4 hours from La Paz.
