# Trekking Routes Near La Paz

## Choro Trail

The Choro Trail is one of the best-known trekking routes in Bolivia. It connects the altiplano near La Cumbre (over 4,700 masl) with the subtropical Yungas region, near the town of Chairo. It's a stretch of pre-Hispanic cobblestone road, originally used to connect the altiplano with coca- and fruit-producing areas.

- **Duration**: 3 to 4 days.
- **Distance**: approximately 70 km.
- **Difficulty**: medium-high, mainly due to the elevation change (more than 3,500 meters of descent).
- **Starting point**: La Cumbre, 30-40 minutes by car from La Paz.
- **End point**: Chairo, from where you can take transport to Coroico.
- **Best season**: May to September (dry season).

## Valle de las Ánimas

A short hike, ideal for half a day, located about 30 minutes from La Paz. It's known for vertical rock formations of eroded clay that resemble spikes or "spirits" (ánimas).

- **Duration**: 2 to 3 hours.
- **Difficulty**: low.
- **Access**: reachable by public transport or taxi from the southern zone of La Paz.

## Muela del Diablo

A hike popular among locals ("paceños") on weekends. It leads to a natural viewpoint with views of the city of La Paz and the Illimani snow-capped peak.

- **Duration**: 3 to 4 hours round trip.
- **Difficulty**: medium, with a rocky final ascent.
- **Access**: southern zone of La Paz, Pedregal neighborhood.
